About

Game design & visual art

Peter Han

I make games about relationships. In To Sever a Cord, I wanted the player to see what the mother and son struggle to say to each other. Designing their rooms, drawing them at different ages, and deciding when the player could act were all ways of telling that story.

Alongside my games, I draw from observation and build 3D models. This gallery brings those studies together with the games they inform.
